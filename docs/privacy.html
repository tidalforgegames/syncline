Privacy Policy

Syncline, by Tidal Forge Games. Last updated 29 August 2026.

Syncline collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind. Everything the app records about you stays on your device, and Tidal Forge Games operates no server for it to be sent to.

What the app stores, and where

Syncline keeps the following on your device only: your scores and run history, your progress through story mode, your settings and audio calibration, any music you import, and the note charts it generates from that music. This is ordinary app storage, in the app's own container. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

What leaves your device

Nothing is sent to Tidal Forge Games, because there is nowhere to send it. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising identifier and no third-party libraries. It does not read your contacts, your photos, your location, your microphone, or your music library.

Music you import is copied into the app so it can be analysed and played. It is never uploaded.

The three things that do involve someone else

Purchases. Syncline offers one optional in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details and we receive no information that identifies you. Whether you own the purchase is answered by Apple's own receipt on your device, which is also why it is restored automatically when you reinstall or move to a new phone.

Links. The Credits screen links to the pages of the musicians whose Creative Commons tracks are included, and to the licence texts. Tapping one opens your browser and leaves the app. Those sites have their own privacy policies and we have no control over them.

Apple's own services. Standard App Store functions such as downloading, updating and, if you have it switched on, iCloud device backup, are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Syncline is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed to appeal primarily to them. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Your data, and getting rid of it

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to hand over, correct or erase on request. Everything the app stores is on your device and under your control: deleting the app removes it. If you want to clear individual songs or charts without deleting the app, Settings › Storage does that.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Syncline changes what it does with data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change. Material changes will also be noted in the App Store release notes.
