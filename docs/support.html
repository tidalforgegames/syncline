Common questions

The notes feel out of time with the music

Open Settings and use Audio latency. Move it right if the music runs ahead of the notes, left if it lags. Bluetooth headphones are the usual cause: they can add a fifth of a second that the phone does not report, and the slider reaches far enough to cancel it. Wired headphones or the phone's own speaker need almost nothing.

I bought the full version and it is locked again

Open the purchase page from Settings and tap Restore purchase. The purchase belongs to your Apple Account rather than to the installed copy, so it survives deleting the app and follows you to a new phone. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Can I play my own music?

Yes, with the full version. Use Import from the ••• menu on the Setlist and pick any DRM-free audio file. Syncline analyses it on your phone and builds four difficulties from what it hears. Music bought from the iTunes Store or streamed from a subscription service is protected and cannot be imported by any app.

A song disappeared, or says it needs analysis

Settings › Storage lists everything the app is holding and lets you free or restore individual songs. Songs included with the app can always be restored without a connection.

Notifications interrupt me mid-song

Settings › Silence interruptions explains how to have Do Not Disturb turn itself on while you play. iOS does not let an app do this by itself, so it is a one-time setup in the Shortcuts app.

The story is too hard, or I picked the wrong difficulty

The story runs at one difficulty for the whole playthrough, chosen at the start. Start over on the Story tab clears that choice along with your progress, which is the only way to change it. Your scores are kept.

Reporting a bug

Please include the song, the difficulty, and whether it happens every time. If the app closed unexpectedly, Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements › Analytics Data on your iPhone contains a file beginning with Syncline; attaching it helps a great deal.

Music

Every track included with Syncline is licensed Creative Commons Attribution and the musicians are credited in the app under Settings › Credits, with links to their pages. If you are one of them and something is wrong, please write and it will be fixed straight away.
